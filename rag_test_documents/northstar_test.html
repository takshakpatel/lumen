Project Northstar Operations Brief

Known answer document for Lumen format testing

Project Northstar is a fictional customer-support pilot for Harbor and Pine. Its purpose is to reduce the time customers wait for a first human response.

Pilot facts

ItemDetails
Pilot launchTuesday, 18 November 2026
Project ownerMira Shah
Operating methodIndigo protocol
Approved budgetINR 480,000
Baseline first response time10 hours
Target first response time7 hours
Success thresholdA 30 percent reduction in first response time

Risks

  • The first risk is that weekend staffing may be too low during the first month.
  • The second risk is that the Indigo protocol may be applied inconsistently by new agents.

Review decision

The operations team will review the pilot after 28 days. If the 7-hour target is met, Harbor and Pine will extend Project Northstar to its returns-support team.
